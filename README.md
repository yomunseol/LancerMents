## Why LancerMents?

Most project management tools are bloated, cluttered, and require constant manual upkeep. LancerMents is different:

-   **Never Chase Payments Again:** Our built-in billing system handles invoicing, smart retries, and automated follow-ups. You just do the work; we get you paid.
-   **Easy on the Eyes:** A premium, high-contrast dark mode designed to reduce eye strain during long work sessions. It is fully accessible and readable for everyone.
-   **Transparent Pricing:** What you see is what you get. Our system automatically enforces your plan limits, so there are no surprise overage fees at the end of the month.
-   **Lightning Fast:** Built for speed. Every click, task update, and calendar change happens instantly.
