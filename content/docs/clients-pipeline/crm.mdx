CRM appears for Pro, Expert, and Business plans. During the public beta the page is a preview: you cannot create or move deals yet.

## What is available

- The CRM screen opens from the sidebar on eligible plans.
- Lower plans see an upgrade card instead of the board.
- Deal stages are defined as Lead, Contacted, Proposal, and Won.

## Where to keep deal context now

Until the board is live, keep the useful details in the client record:

1. Open **Clients** and choose the account.
2. Add a note with the current stage, next step, and expected value.
3. Add the follow-up as a task with a due date.

That keeps the information searchable today and easy to move into the CRM board later.
