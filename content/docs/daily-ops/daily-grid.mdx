The daily grid is deliberately small. You should be able to read it in a glance and act in one tap.

## The cap keeps the day honest

On the free tier you can hold **five open tasks** at once. The pills next to *Daily grid* fill as you add them. When the grid is full, the add button locks and a quiet line appears telling you The Pipeline unlocks more. Nothing already on your board is ever hidden or deleted — the cap only blocks *creating* a new open task.

## Three sections, one order

Your tasks sort themselves into **Today**, **Scheduled**, and **Completed**.

- *Today* holds anything due today, overdue, or with no date at all.
- *Scheduled* holds anything due in the future, earliest first.
- *Completed* stays folded away behind a count until you open it.

## Due dates are optional

Type a task and hit enter, and it lands in Today. Want it later? Pick a date from the date picker before you add it, and it moves to Scheduled. Check a task off and it slides into Completed with the others, struck through and dimmed.
