Tasks live on the daily grid. The dashboard and the Tasks page show the same list, so you can plan from either place.

## Add a task

1. Type the task title in the field under the grid.
2. Pick a due date if the task has one.
3. Press the add button or hit Enter.

## Work the grid

- **Today** collects tasks with no due date and tasks due today.
- **Scheduled** lists tasks with a future due date.
- **Completed** stays collapsed until you open it.
- Tick the checkbox to complete a task. The row moves to Completed.
- The small X on a row deletes it.

## Plan limits

Starter supports five active tasks at a time. Completed tasks do not count toward the limit. When the grid is full, the add button locks, but every task already on your board stays visible. Higher plans remove the cap.
