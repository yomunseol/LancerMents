Profile, Security, and Settings cover three different jobs. Profile is about you, Security is about access, and Settings is about the workspace.

## Update your profile

1. Open **Profile** from the sidebar.
2. Edit your display name and choose the business type that fits your work.
3. Review your current plan. Use **Change plan** to move to another tier.
4. Pick your language from the list.

Invoice defaults and currency fields are not part of the beta yet. Amounts currently display in USD.

## Protect your account

The **Security** page holds login protection:

- Two-factor authentication can be off, use an emailed code, or use an authenticator app.
- The session list shows device, browser, operating system, approximate location, and last activity.
- Revoke any session you do not recognize, or sign out of all other sessions.
- Change your password from the same page. You will be asked for the current one first.

## Adjust workspace preferences

**Settings** covers the workspace itself:

1. Choose light, dark, or system appearance.
2. Pick the workspace that should open by default.
3. Rename the active workspace.
4. Use the danger zone to sign out of every device.
