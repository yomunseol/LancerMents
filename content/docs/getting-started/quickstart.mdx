LancerMents starts you with a workspace that already exists. You can rename it later, so you do not need to plan your structure on day one.

## Create your account

1. Open **Sign up** and enter your email and password twice.
2. Pick a password rated **Moderate** or better. The button stays locked below that.
3. Check your inbox for a 6-digit code and enter it.
4. Choose the work type that fits you, then pick a plan. Every plan is free during the public beta.

## Switch workspaces

The workspace switcher lives in the dashboard sidebar. It lists every workspace you own and remembers your last choice on this device. Rename the active workspace in **Settings** if you want a clearer label.

## Find your way around

The sidebar links to Tasks, Notes, Calendar, Clients, CRM, Invoices, Analytics, Spreadsheet, Profile, Security, and Settings. Items with a lock need a higher plan. The dashboard shows your daily task grid plus a shortcut to change plans.
