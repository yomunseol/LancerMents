You are two short steps away from a working workspace. Neither one asks you to configure anything you cannot change later.

## Create your account

Open **Sign up** and enter your email address, a password, and the same password again. LancerMents shows a strength bar under the field: you need **Moderate** or better before the button unlocks. A password like `Password1` is enough; a long phrase with a number and a symbol is better.

## Confirm with the code we email you

We send a **6-digit code** to your inbox — not a link you have to hunt for. Type it into the code field. If the email is slow, press **Resend code**; the button rests for thirty seconds so you cannot send a dozen of them by accident.

## Finish the wizard

Verifying the code signs you in and drops you into a two-step wizard. First, tell us what best describes your work so the workspace fits you. Second, pick a plan — everything is free during Beta, so nothing is charged. From there you land on your dashboard with a workspace already created.
