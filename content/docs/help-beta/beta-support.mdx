LancerMents is in an open beta. Features are still changing, and the person reading your email is the person building the product.

## What is free

- Every plan is free during the public beta.
- No payment method is required.
- Before any paid billing starts, the product will show the price and billing period first.

## Export or delete your data

There is no self-serve export screen yet. Email **support@lancerments.online** and say whether you want an export or a deletion. We aim to reply within five business days.

## Report a bug or request a feature

1. Email **support@lancerments.online**.
2. Include the page, what you expected, and what happened.
3. Add a screenshot if the problem is visual.

Feature requests are read the same way. Short, specific emails are easiest to act on.
