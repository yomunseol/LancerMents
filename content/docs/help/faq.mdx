## Is it really free right now?

Yes. Every plan is free during Beta. You are never asked for a card, and there is nothing to cancel.

## What happens to my tasks if I stay on the free tier?

Nothing bad. The free tier caps how many *open* tasks you can create at five, but every task you already have stays exactly where it is.

## Do I need an authenticator app?

No. You can leave two-factor off, use a code emailed to you, or bring an authenticator app — your choice, switchable any time from Security.

## Can I get back into a session I signed out?

No. Revoking a session signs that browser out immediately and it cannot be re-opened silently. Sign in again and you get a fresh one.

## Where are my notes kept?

In your workspace, tied to your account. They are plain text — no folders, no rich-text traps — so they stay readable and easy to export.

## How do I change my plan?

Open **Profile** and press *Change plan*. The wizard remembers where you are and drops you straight onto plan selection.
