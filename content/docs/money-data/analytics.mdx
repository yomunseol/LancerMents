Analytics appears on Pro, Expert, and Business plans. During the public beta the page is a preview, so charts and totals are not live yet.

## What is available

- The module opens from the sidebar on eligible plans.
- Lower plans see an upgrade card.
- The planned view covers monthly revenue, unpaid balances, tracked hours, and a client breakdown.

## Check numbers today

1. Open a client detail page.
2. Read the invoice list to see what is paid, sent, or overdue.
3. Check the daily grid for current workload.

These two views are the reliable numbers during beta. The dashboard in Analytics will replace the manual check when it ships.
