Invoices are visible on Pro, Expert, and Business plans. The beta focuses on keeping invoice history next to the client while editing tools are finished.

## See a client's invoices

1. Open **Clients** from the sidebar.
2. Select a client name to open the detail page, or open the drawer from the list.
3. Find the **Invoices** section. Each row shows the amount and payment status.

## Read the statuses

- **Draft** means the invoice has not been sent.
- **Sent** means it left your hands and is waiting.
- **Paid** means the money arrived.
- **Overdue** means the due date passed without payment.

## Current limits

Creating an invoice, sending it, and exporting a PDF are not live during the beta. Amounts display in USD. Use your existing invoicing tool for now and record the status here so the client view stays accurate.
